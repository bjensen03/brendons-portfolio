## The idea

Four variables set the price of an event space: the date, the time slot, the
type of space and the head count. Change any one and the total moves.

Which is why the booking summary is on screen while they are being chosen
rather than revealed at the end. A checkout that surprises someone with the
real number after they have filled in their guest list is not a checkout, it is
a negotiation they did not agree to.
