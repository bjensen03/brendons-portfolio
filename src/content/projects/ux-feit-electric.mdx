## The idea

A smart bulb is registered as a device, so most apps for them show you a list of
devices. That is the manufacturer's model, not the customer's. Nobody stands in
their kitchen thinking about a paired endpoint; they think *kitchen*.

So the primary screen here is rooms, each showing what is on and what it is
drawing. Devices still exist — you need them to pair hardware and to fix things
— but they sit behind a tab rather than in front of the person who just wants
the pendants off.

The other decision worth naming is that consumption is a first-class screen
rather than a settings page. A smart bulb's whole commercial argument is that it
costs less to run, and an app that never shows you the number is not making the
argument it was bought on.
