## The idea

The failure mode when booking a photographer is not the software, it is the
sequence: availability lives in one message, the rate in another, and the work
somewhere else entirely. Any one of those going unanswered ends the booking.

So availability, portfolio and rate sit together. A listing shows the work, the
review count and the slots that are actually open, and the booking screen
confirms date, time, photographer and contact in one card rather than as a
conversation to be reconstructed later.

The schedule view is the other half of the marketplace — the photographer's own
day, with what has been booked against it — because a two-sided product that
only designs one side is half a product.
